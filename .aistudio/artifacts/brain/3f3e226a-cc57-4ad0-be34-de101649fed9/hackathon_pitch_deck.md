### Slide 3: The Root Cause — Why Cheap Internet Didn't Fix This

#### 1. Slide Title & Subtitle
- **Title:** The Root Cause: The Smartphone Paradox
- **Subtitle:** Citizens Don't Lack Devices or Data — Government Portals Were Built for Clerks, Not Citizens

#### 2. Bullet Points (On-Slide Text)
- **Desktop-Only Architecture:** Portals were architected for 1080p desktop monitors, Windows XP, and flatbed optical scanners—not vertical 6-inch smartphone touchscreens.
- **Brick Wall 1 — Administrative Jargon:** Unexplained legal Latin & bureaucratic terms (*"Deponent"*, *"Proforma"*, *"Annexure 1A"*, *"Sub-judice"*, *"Non-Judicial Stamp"*).
- **Brick Wall 2 — Strict File Size Envelopes:** Mandates like *"Photo 10–200 KB JPG"* or *"Signature 10–100 KB"*; smartphone cameras natively capture 4–8 MB HEIC/JPG files with zero native resizing tools.
- **Brick Wall 3 — Cryptic Rejections:** Opaque red errors (*"HTTP 502"*, *"Invalid schema payload"*, *"Session timed out"*) that cause panic and push citizens right back to cyber cafes.

#### 3. Visual & Layout Instructions for Designer
- **Layout:** "The 3 Brick Walls" Infographic diagram showing a citizen holding a smartphone hitting three consecutive barriers before reaching the government server.
  - *Wall 1 Icon:* Confusing document icon labeled *"Jargon (Deponent? Proforma?)"*.
  - *Wall 2 Icon:* Red rejection badge labeled *"File Exceeds 200 KB — Upload Failed"*.
  - *Wall 3 Icon:* Session timeout dialog labeled *"Session Expired. Token Mismatch"*.
- **Graphic Accent:** Contrast circle showing a smartphone camera capturing an 8 MB high-res photo versus a portal rejecting anything above 200 KB.

#### 4. Presenter Stage Pitch Script (30 Seconds)
> *"Why didn't 300 million Jio connections kill the cyber cafe? Because having a smartphone doesn't mean you can pass a government portal's barriers. Every citizen hits three brick walls: First, archaic bureaucratic jargon like 'Deponent' or 'Form 1A'. Second, impossible technical gates—the portal rejects any photo above 200 KB, but a phone shoots at 5 Megabytes! And third, cryptic error codes that scare people into thinking they ruined their application. Cyber cafes don't sell internet anymore; they sell file resizing and jargon translation."*

### Slide 7: System Architecture & Anti-Hallucination Design

#### 1. Slide Title & Subtitle
- **Title:** System Architecture: Zero-Hallucination Civic AI
- **Subtitle:** Engineering Reliability Where Hallucination is Not an Option

#### 2. Bullet Points (On-Slide Text)
- **Frontend Architecture:** React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS with high-contrast accessibility, responsive viewport design, and zero-pill discipline.
- **Local Processing Utility:** HTML5 Canvas image manipulation engine for zero-latency, private, client-side document resizing.
- **Deterministic AI Core:** Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite API (Multimodal Text & Vision) running with strict configuration: **Temperature = 0.2**.
- **Deterministic Grounding Guardrail:** The model is locked to our verified, pre-audited government knowledge base. If an unsupported query or fraudulent portal is queried, the system deflects immediately—preventing invented fees, fake links, or incorrect portal steps.

#### 3. Visual & Layout Instructions for Designer
- **Layout:** Architectural schematic flow diagram (Left to Right):
  1. *Client Layer:* Smartphone Browser (React 19 + HTML5 Canvas Resizer).
  2. *Secure Express API Gateway:* Rate-limited backend proxy (`/api/chat`, `/api/analyze-screenshot`) with injected runtime secrets.
  3. *AI Engine:* Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (Temp = 0.2, Multimodal Vision).
  4. *Grounding Knowledge Vault:* Hardcoded, audited templates for all 9 government procedures enforcing strict URL & fee boundaries.
- **Visual Accent:** Lock icon over the Knowledge Vault with label: *"Zero Hallucination Guarantee: No unverified internet scraping"*.

#### 4. Presenter Stage Pitch Script (30 Seconds)
> *"In a government portal, if an AI hallucinates a link or invents a fee, a citizen loses money or misses an exam deadline. That's why we engineered an anti-hallucination architecture. We run Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash model with temperature locked at 0.2, strictly bounded by our deterministic verified knowledge base. The AI cannot invent portal steps, fake fees, or unverified URLs. Combined with our client-side canvas compressor, our architecture is lightning-fast, privacy-preserving, and rock-solid dependable."*
